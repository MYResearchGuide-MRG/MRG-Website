## Programme years

Demo Day editions are configured in `src/data/demo-day.ts`. The 2026 panel is stored separately from future panels. Add a new edition with its year, main award judges, special award judges and awards notice when it is ready to publish. Year tabs are generated automatically, newest first; do not overwrite an older edition's biographies with a future panel.

Links use `#/judges/2026` and `#/awards/2026`. Switching between Judges and Awards preserves the selected year. The old `#/judges` and `#/awards` links open the latest published edition. Unknown years show an unpublished message rather than another cohort's judges. These editions are maintained in the codebase, not the participant administration panel.
